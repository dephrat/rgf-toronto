Continuing Ryan’s Legacy. Inspiring Hope.

Founded in honour of Ryan Gordy, whose life and spirit continue to inspire our purpose, the Ryan Gordy Foundation is dedicated to supporting young adults navigating cancer treatment.

In partnership with Princess Margaret Cancer Centre’s Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Oncology Program, we provide meaningful support through financial assistance, wellness, and mental health initiatives — bringing comfort, strength, and hope to young adults and their families.

All funds raised in Toronto stay in the Toronto community.
